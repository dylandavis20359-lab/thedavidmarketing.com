Privacy

How we handle your info

This page covers the contact form on thedavidmarketing.com. In plain English:

  • The form asks for your name, business name, email, and optionally your phone number, your page link and a message. Only your name, business name, email and a goal are required.
  • Submitting the form sends that information to FormSubmit, a third-party form-delivery service, which emails it straight to us at thedavidmarketing@outlook.com. We do not run our own server or database, and no other company or advertiser gets a copy.
  • We use it for exactly one thing: replying about your free page check. We do not sell it, rent it, or add you to any list you did not ask for.
  • The form also has a hidden field real visitors never see. It exists to catch spam bots, not to collect anything from you.
  • Want your info deleted from our inbox? Message us on Instagram at @thedavidmarketing or email thedavidmarketing@outlook.com and we will remove it.
