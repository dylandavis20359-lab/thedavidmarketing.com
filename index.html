Free tips

Three things you can do with your page today

1

Post on a real schedule

Three posts a week, every week, beats ten posts in one burst. Steady posting is what builds reach.

2

Make it worth sharing

Before you post, ask: would someone send this to a friend? Helpful and fun posts travel further than ads.

3

Reply the same day

A fast, friendly reply is often the difference between a question and a booking.
